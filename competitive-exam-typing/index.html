Competitive Exam Typing Practice — SSC, CPCT, UP Police

Free typing practice for SSC CHSL, SSC CGL, CPCT, UP Police, court clerk and railway exams. Real exam duration, WPM and accuracy — in English and Hindi.

Government typing exams in India each have their own speed requirement, duration and language rules, and mixing them up is one of the most common preparation mistakes. This page covers real practice formats for SSC CHSL (35 WPM English / 30 WPM Hindi, 10 minutes), SSC CGL's DEST (2,000 key depressions in 15 minutes), CPCT (Hindi Unicode Mangal, not Kruti Dev), UP Police (commonly Kruti Dev), Railway NTPC's Typing Skill Test, and court clerk and stenographer exams, among others. Each test here reproduces the exact duration and scoring method of its real exam rather than a generic timer, because a 10-minute CHSL-style test and a 15-minute CGL DEST test measure genuinely different things — one is words per minute, the other is key depressions per hour. Since exam requirements do change between notification cycles, always confirm the current speed, duration and Hindi layout (Mangal vs Kruti Dev) from your own exam's official notification before you start preparing, and use these practice tests to build toward that specific number rather than a generic target.
