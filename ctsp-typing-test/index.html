CTSP Typing Test Online — Chhattisgarh Typing Practice

Free CTSP (Chhattisgarh Computer Typing Skill Test) practice — Hindi and English typing for 5000/8000/10000 KDPH speed levels, with live WPM and accuracy.

CTSP is run by the Shorthand and Typing Computer Skills Examination Council, established under the Directorate of Public Instruction within Chhattisgarh's Department of School Education, to certify computer typing proficiency for Class III government posts in the state. It's a certification you can earn independent of a specific job application — similar in spirit to MP's CPCT — and conducts separate Hindi and English Computer Typing Skill Tests, plus Stenography Examinations, at three distinct speed levels: 5,000, 8,000 and 10,000 key depressions per hour (KDPH). Each speed level in each language is effectively its own separate application and certificate, so passing at 8,000 KDPH English doesn't automatically certify you at 10,000, or in Hindi.

Converting KDPH to a more familiar WPM figure: 8,000 KDPH works out to roughly 133 key depressions per minute, or about 27 WPM at five characters per word — in the same range as SSC's DEST and most DEO-track typing tests. The 5,000 KDPH level is correspondingly easier (about 83 per minute, roughly 17 WPM), while 10,000 KDPH is noticeably harder (about 167 per minute, roughly 33 WPM) — worth knowing before you register for a specific level, since the jump from 8,000 to 10,000 is a real step up, not a minor increment.

CTSP has moved to a fully online platform (ctsp.cg.nic.in) built by NIC Chhattisgarh, described as "Skill Test as a Service" and handling registration, fee payment and slot allocation digitally rather than through paper applications — a relatively recent change, so if you've seen older guidance describing a different application process, it likely predates this platform.

Since CTSP certifies a specific KDPH level rather than a general "pass," the practical preparation question is which level your target post actually requires — some Chhattisgarh government posts for Data Entry Operator, Assistant Grade-3 and Stenographer roles have required different levels, and applying for a certificate at a lower level than your post needs means reapplying and retesting at the correct one. Confirm the required KDPH level from your specific post's recruitment notice before registering for a CTSP test level.

Registering for a KDPH level based on convenience rather than requirement is an easy way to waste an attempt — registering for the easier 5,000 KDPH level when your target post actually requires 8,000 means a wasted application and test slot, since CTSP certifies the specific level you registered and tested for, not a general pass. Confirm the exact level your post needs before registering, rather than defaulting to whichever level feels most immediately achievable.

CTSP's exact fee, registration process and any level-specific accuracy requirement are managed through the ctsp.cg.nic.in platform and can change, so that site is the one to check for your current cycle's details. The free CTSP practice above lets you build toward any of the three KDPH levels in Hindi or English in the meantime, with live speed and accuracy tracking so you can see your pace in KDPH terms directly rather than converting from WPM each time.
